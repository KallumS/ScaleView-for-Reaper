# 2026-11-01 - maj7(sus2), measured before deciding (decision 0014)

The local session played 37 chords into REAPER and read them with Pro and
Scaler Detector at once. Of the 15 places they differ, one group looked like
Pro being wrong: its slash put its own added 11 in the bass. This session
brought the evidence up to date, measured letting Pro name maj7(sus2) - not a
Starting Blocks chord type - and recommends keeping it.

## Step 0: the data

`tools/scaler_cases.py` and `CLAUDE.md` taken from the local session's copy:
34 probe rows (58 cases in all), and the C minor D F G# A# row's bass corrected
from F to D. The prompt's block of rows ended one short of the local file; the
file's last row, C E F G over F, is the second target chord and is kept.

## The tools were broken on `main`

`tools/runner.lua` mocked `gfx` without `getfont`, which the script calls since
1.2.0 (`pickFace()`). Both test suites had been taught it; the runner had not.
Every tool built on it - `scaler_cases.py`, `sweep.py`, `jazznet_check.py`,
`leadsheet_check.py`, `wikipedia_chords.py` - printed an empty name for every
chord, and `scaler_cases.py` reported 0/58 without complaint. Fixed first, in
its own commit. Worth remembering alongside *profile the harness*: a tool that
fails by printing nothing looks like a result.

## Baseline

Every "Pro ..." note in the new rows, read off the screen in REAPER, matches
what the script prints - all 34, checked mechanically.

## The change

`{0,2,7,11}` accepted as a root beside Blocks' types in `blocksRoots`. Two
variants were built: offered on any root (A), and only when its root is the
bass (B), which is Scaler's rule stated directly. **They name every voicing
that can contain the shape - all 48, in all 288 keys - identically**, because
the cost already keeps the add11 in the other three positions. A was kept as
the smaller change.

**Lead sheets**, one shape: the arranger's `Eb/Ab` (Eb G Bb over Ab, 11

exactly the shape being changed - Eb's 11 in the bass - so it cannot be
avoided without giving the gain back. It was `Abmaj7sus2` before decision 0011
too, and CLAUDE.md's lead-sheet section still cited it that way: 0011's whole
lead-sheet gain was this one shape.

## The test suite

One break, in both variants: the pin `C D G B` -> `Gadd11/C`, one of 0011's
six moved names. It is not a regression - it is G's 11 in the bass, the
pattern this change exists for - so the expectation changes back to
`Cmaj7sus2`. Five new expectations: the two Scaler targets, and the add11
held over its third, its fifth and its root. The three that change fail on the
old code; the suite passes on the new.

## The D E G A / E G A family

Undisturbed, and it cannot be otherwise. The exception matches exactly four
notes in one shape; no rotation of D E G A or E G A is `{0,2,7,11}`.
maj7(sus2) is a core quality (`sus2/P/maj7`, rank 20), so `COST_SUS_EXTRA`
never applies to it, and nothing about suspensions carrying added tones moved.
The three Scaler rows of that family read as before.

## Recommendation: keep

- It fixes the one group of Scaler differences where Pro looked wrong, and
  nothing else: 12 voicings per key, no Scaler losses, jazznet and Wikipedia
  untouched, 100% exact throughout.
- The lead-sheet loss is the same shape seen from the other side. `Eb/Ab` is
  an arranger writing a triad over a bass; `Ebadd11/Ab` was not that either -
  it claims the Ab is Eb's 11. `Abmaj7sus2` names the notes as one chord, the
  same treatment as `Fm7/Bb` reading `Bb11` in that section.
- The cost is to Blocks agreement: Blocks' add11 voiced over its 11 is now
  named on a root Blocks does not use. Not measured here (it needs Noterator's
  Blocks toolbox); it is one voicing of one chord type.
